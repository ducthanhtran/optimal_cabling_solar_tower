\begin{frame}{Conclusion}
\vfill
\begin{itemize}
  \item MST local search procedure too restrictive
  \begin{itemize}
    \item have to consider up- and downgrades of power cables
    \item switch costs eat up a lot of budget!
    \item for more than 5 partitions we obtain more degrees of freedom, thus local search improvements are much higher
  \end{itemize}
  \item[]
  \item Hamilton local search $\to$ greedy power cable assignment yield best results
  \item[]
  \item considering both as seperate problems might be also beneficial
  \begin{itemize}
    \item local search is not too restrictive
    \item power cabling problem $\equiv$ multilevel capacitated minimum spanning tree\footnote{Gamvros et al.: The multilevel capacitated minimum spanning tree problem, INFORMS Journal on Computing, 2006}
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}
